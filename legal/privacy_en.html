3. Categories of personal data processed

Depending on the configuration and use of the Software, the following may be processed:

  • Activation and licensing data: license identifier, activation status, technical device identifiers where necessary for license validation.
  • Professional contact data: name, company name, business email address, supplied by the User for the purchase of the license, invoicing or support.
  • Data contained in diagnostic bundles: component versions, technical configuration, application logs, recent errors, execution-environment information. Such bundles are generated locally at the User's initiative and transmitted to support only if the User decides to send them.

These credentials are stored locally where technically implemented (for example, via Windows Credential Manager) and are not transmitted to the Licensor in the normal operation of the Software.

  • Activation and verification of the validity of the license.
  • Management of the contractual relationship, invoicing and tax/accounting obligations.
  • Technical support, handling of support requests and management of malfunction reports through diagnostic bundles transmitted by the User.
  • Compliance with legal obligations, handling of disputes and protection of the Licensor's rights.
  • Security of the Software, prevention of unauthorised use and protection of intellectual property.

5. Legal bases of processing

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of the license agreement and pre-contractual measures (supply of the Software, activation, support).

  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR — consent, where required for specific optional features.

8. Diagnostics and support

The Software includes a function to export a diagnostic bundle generated locally. Where technically implemented, the bundle is designed to exclude market data and to mask secrets (keys, tokens, passwords). The bundle is created solely at the User's initiative, remains under the User's control and is transmitted to support only if the User decides to send it.

The User is required to review the content of the diagnostic bundle before sending it to support, in order to exclude market data, unnecessary personal data and any secrets that may not have been masked. Bundles received are processed by the Licensor solely for technical-support purposes.

Where the User configures an external service, the content transmitted to that service is subject to the terms and privacy notice of the chosen provider and may involve transfers of data outside the European Economic Area, depending on the provider configured by the User. The selection and configuration of such services remain under the sole responsibility of the User.

Telegram is a third-party service, subject to its own privacy notice. The User is responsible for the management of the bot token and the chat ID, for the use of the channel and for the compliance of transmitted content with applicable law and any confidentiality obligations.
