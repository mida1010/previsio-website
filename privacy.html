The Software is a professional product intended for business/professional users (for example: asset managers, investment firms, management companies, financial advisors, professional firms, family offices, institutional/professional clients).

For personal data matters, the User may write to previsio.quant@gmail.com. 2. Scope of this notice

This notice applies to any personal data processed in connection with the installation, activation, use of and support for the Software. It does not apply to personal data that the User independently decides to process through the Software (for example, datasets uploaded that contain personal data). With respect to such data, the User acts as an independent controller and is required to provide data subjects with its own notice and to comply with all GDPR obligations.

3. Categories of personal data processed

Depending on the configuration and use of the Software, the following may be processed:

  • Activation and licensing data: license identifier, activation status, technical device identifiers where necessary for license validation.
  • Professional contact data: name, company name, business email address, supplied by the User for the purchase of the license, invoicing or support.
  • Data contained in diagnostic bundles: component versions, technical configuration, application logs, recent errors, execution-environment information. Such bundles are generated locally at the User's initiative and transmitted to support only if the User decides to send them.
  • Credentials and secrets configured by the User: for example Telegram bot token and chat ID, credentials required by supported optional features. These credentials are stored locally where technically implemented (for example, via Windows Credential Manager) and are not transmitted to the Licensor in the normal operation of the Software.
  • Data possibly contained in datasets uploaded by the User: time series, input and output files. Such data resides on the User's device and is not transmitted to the Licensor in the normal operation of the Software.

4. Purposes of processing

  • Supply, installation and operation of the Software.
  • Activation and verification of the validity of the license.
  • Management of the contractual relationship, invoicing and tax/accounting obligations.
  • Technical support, handling of support requests and management of malfunction reports through diagnostic bundles transmitted by the User.
  • Compliance with legal obligations, handling of disputes and protection of the Licensor's rights.
  • Security of the Software, prevention of unauthorised use and protection of intellectual property.

5. Legal bases of processing

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of the license agreement and pre-contractual measures (supply of the Software, activation, support).
  • Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR — compliance with legal obligations (tax, accounting, security).
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest of the Licensor in protecting the Software, preventing abuse, ensuring security and defending legal claims.
  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR — consent, where required for specific optional features.

6. Local processing and User-controlled data

The Software is designed to operate primarily locally on the User's device. By default, market datasets and files uploaded by the User are processed locally and are not transmitted to the Licensor, unless the User voluntarily decides to send diagnostic bundles or support materials, or configures external services described in the following sections.

The Licensor does not access datasets uploaded by the User in the normal operation of the Software. The User is required to ensure that uploaded datasets do not contain personal data unless the User has a valid legal basis and is authorised to process such data. The User also remains responsible for compliance with market-data rights, third-party data-provider licenses (for example, Bloomberg or other market data providers) and any redistribution restrictions.

7. License activation and validation

Where a license-key mechanism is active, the minimum data necessary to verify the validity of the license may be processed (for example: license identifier, activation status, technical device identifiers). No further detail document currently applies beyond this notice; this section will be updated if a dedicated activation-data annex is introduced.

8. Diagnostics and support

The Software includes a function to export a diagnostic bundle generated locally. Where technically implemented, the bundle is designed to exclude market data and to mask secrets (keys, tokens, passwords). The bundle is created solely at the User's initiative, remains under the User's control and is transmitted to support only if the User decides to send it.

The User is required to review the content of the diagnostic bundle before sending it to support, in order to exclude market data, unnecessary personal data and any secrets that may not have been masked. Bundles received are processed by the Licensor solely for technical-support purposes.

9. Local AI commentary

The Microsoft Store desktop version may generate narrative commentary through Ollama running locally on the User's device. Market datasets, quantitative results and report content used by this function are not sent to external AI providers. Remote LLM endpoints and third-party cloud AI providers are not supported by the standard Microsoft Store product.

The language model produces qualitative narrative commentary only. Forecasts, probabilities, metrics, formulas and all other numerical results are calculated by the Software's quantitative engine before the commentary stage.

10. Optional Telegram delivery

The Software may allow the delivery of notifications/reports via Telegram. If the User configures this feature, the User must provide a Telegram bot token and a chat ID. Telegram is a third-party service, subject to its own privacy notice. The User is responsible for the management of the bot token and the chat ID, for the use of the channel and for the compliance of transmitted content with applicable law and any confidentiality obligations.

11. Local storage of credentials and secrets

Where technically implemented, credentials and secrets configured by the User (Telegram bot token, API keys of optional services, other credentials) are stored locally using operating system mechanisms (for example, Windows Credential Manager). Such secrets must not be included in clear text in diagnostic bundles, and, where technically implemented, the Software applies masking. The User remains responsible for the security of the User's device and of the credentials stored.

12. Recipients and processors

Personal data may be shared with parties acting as processors appointed under Article 28 GDPR (for example: hosting providers, license-activation services, accounting systems, email providers) and with authorised persons (Licensor's personnel). The list of processors is available upon request by writing to previsio.quant@gmail.com.

Third-party services that the User independently decides to configure (cloud LLM, Telegram, etc.) do not act as processors of the Licensor and are governed by their own contracts and privacy notices.

13. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

The Licensor prefers providers located within the European Economic Area. Where transfers to third countries are necessary, they will be carried out subject to appropriate safeguards under Articles 44 and following of the GDPR (for example: adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses). Details: no non-EEA transfer is currently carried out by the Licensor; this section will be updated if that changes. Transfers arising from third-party services configured by the User (cloud LLM, Telegram, etc.) are governed by the respective providers.

14. Retention periods

Data is retained for the time strictly necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and, in any case, in accordance with legal obligations (for example: retention of accounting and tax documents for the period required by applicable law). Diagnostic bundles transmitted to support are retained for the time necessary to handle the request and subsequently deleted or anonymised. Details: accounting and tax records are retained for 10 years as required by Italian law; diagnostic bundles are retained only for the time needed to handle the relevant support request and in any case no longer than 12 months. Data processed locally by the User remains on the User's device according to the User's choices.

15. Security measures

The Licensor adopts technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including, by way of example, access control, minimisation principles, masking of secrets in diagnostic bundles where technically implemented, and local storage of credentials via operating system mechanisms. The Licensor does not guarantee the absolute absence of security incidents, and the security of the User's execution environment remains under the User's responsibility.

Data subjects may exercise, within the limits provided by the GDPR, the rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to withdraw any consent given, without prejudice to the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand. Requests may be submitted to the controller at previsio.quant@gmail.com.

17. Right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority

Data subjects have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) if they consider that the processing of their personal data infringes applicable law.

18. Relationship with EULA and Terms of Use

This notice supplements and does not replace the End User License Agreement (EULA) and the Terms of Use of the Software. In case of conflict between this notice and legal obligations on the protection of personal data, the latter prevail.

19. Updates to this notice
